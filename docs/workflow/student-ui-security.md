## Verdict and severity-ranked findings

No confirmed Critical, High, Medium or Low vulnerability was identified in the reviewed Student UI changes. No source-level role elevation, cross-borrower read, policy override or trusted-storage bypass was found. Authenticated runtime acceptance remains blocked/unverified; a passing model or adapter test does not establish HTTP/database enforcement.

Informational data-minimization observation: `database/023_basic_lending.sql:17` includes `hodId` in the policy DTO although the student policy rendering uses name/policy only. `database/039_physical_lifecycle.sql:67` includes allocation, asset and serial references in borrower physical DTOs. These are existing authorized contracts, not evidence of another borrower's disclosure. Prefer explicit role-specific DTO projections when evolving these contracts; opaque identifiers alone are not credentials. Broader inspection/maintenance DTOs use raw record serialization (`039:68-70`); their field minimization merits separate CP3 review, and is not certified by this Student UI review.
