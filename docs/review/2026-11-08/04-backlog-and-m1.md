## M1 approval scope

**Recommended milestone: Foundation and contract validation, 1–2 active engineering weeks.** Estimate assumes resolved review access and sequential specialist availability; it is not a delivery commitment. This proposal does not start M1.

Inputs: approved baseline architecture and SRS with accepted review corrections; policy decision ledger with accepted versus deferred values; named independent review path. P15 must either supply the institution's supported identity constraints or explicitly allow an ADR comparison without choosing/connecting a live provider. P17 production decisions can remain deferred; provisional capacity/recovery assumptions stay visibly unapproved.

Outputs:

1. ADRs for supported compatible versions, package manager, authentication/session strategy and DB integrity/grants approach. Check current official documentation/advisories at execution time, before installing dependencies.
2. Local-only scaffold with strict TypeScript, reproducible lockfile, lint/typecheck/build commands, synthetic fixture definitions and selected test harness. No login implementation, operational CRUD, approvals, inventory mutation or lending features.
3. Reviewed schema/migration and grants **design**, explicit invariants, API command contracts, authorization/action matrix and retry/error semantics. Executable business migrations and live database setup remain for separately authorized subsequent work unless M1 scope is amended.
4. Annotated core desktop/mobile wireframes and component/state inventory; proposed usability/accessibility acceptance mapped to T16.
5. ASVS applicability decisions for selected M1 architecture, threat-model updates, synthetic test scenarios and evidence recording templates. Feature-dependent applicability can stay blocked until its decision; no pass status is fabricated.

Exit gate: B01–B07 delivered and traceable; scaffold checks pass with exact commands/environment recorded; no secrets/real data/external connection introduced; all P0 contract gaps have either approved resolution or an explicit downstream blocker; schema/contract and wireframe reviewers sign; critical/high design findings resolved. Independent reviews cannot be replaced by the lead's self-review. A missing reviewer blocks integration/exit, not a claim that the work passed. Demonstrate synthetic artifacts and obtain acceptance before M2.

Excluded: application features, real student information, live IdP/mail/university connections, cloud CI integration, bot provisioning, deployment, automatic merge, scheduled jobs, production credentials or public publishing. M1 approval is not approval of every P01–P21 default or of M2 onward.

Suggested bounded approval record: “Approve M1 Foundation and contract validation as scoped in docs/review/2026-11-08/04-backlog-and-m1.md, including local dependency installation and scaffold only. Accept the retained SRS/architecture with corrections [IDs]; policy choices accepted [IDs/values], remaining choices deferred with blockers. Independent reviewer [person/approved role]. No business features, external integration or deployment authorized.” The user may narrow this scope; nothing has been authorized by writing this example.
